Privacy Policy for Streamlet

Last updated: November 7, 2025

White Lodge Technologies ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Streamlet mobile application. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Account Email: When you sign in using Google OAuth authentication
  • Reading Preferences: Articles you read, save, or mark as favorites
  • Feed Subscriptions: The RSS feeds you subscribe to
  • Usage Data: App usage statistics to improve service quality

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide personalized RSS feed experience
  • To sync your preferences and reading history across devices
  • To improve app functionality and user experience
  • To communicate important updates about the service

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Your data is stored securely on our servers at taranezy.ddns.net
  • We use HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes

4. Third-Party Services

Google OAuth: We use Google's authentication service to verify your identity. When you sign in with Google, we only receive your email address. Your Google password is never shared with us.

Please review Google's Privacy Policy for information about their data practices.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all data we have about you
  • Correction: Update or correct your information
  • Deletion: Request complete deletion of your account and data
  • Export: Download your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

6. Data Retention

  • We retain your data while your account is active

7. Children's Privacy

Streamlet is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:
